## Direction contract

THESIS: Every Gadai loan is a filed instrument in a public registry of pledged fee rights: numbered form boxes, a registry number, a violet filing stamp that moves as the lien moves (APPROVED, AUCTION, ACTIVE, RELEASED). Refuses the neon-on-black crypto dashboard and the cream-paper editorial with a hard offset shadow (the incumbent look).

OWN-WORLD: Cool grey desk ground (not cream), white bond form sheets with a soft real shadow, 1px slate hairline rules forming shared-edge form boxes, small condensed caption per box, typed entries in Chivo Mono, titles in Archivo at expanded width and heavy weight. Stamp violet is the single brand ink: status stamps, primary actions, focus, selection. Repaid green and declined red are semantic only.

STORY: Visitor reads the pledge, auction, disburse, repay, release track in one line, sees the live registry ledger under it, and either applies or funds a note.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, heavy expanded h1 "Borrow USDC against your token's creator fees" with one sentence and two actions (Apply, Fund a FeeNote). Right, the desk registration box. Full width under it: the five-box lifecycle track, then the registry summary strip, then the ledger.

FORM: candidate 5 of 7 (lien registry filing), seed 38a51a78. Raises: airport wayfinding (next-step band on the loan page), nixie counter (fixed decimal places, right-aligned tabular amounts), CRT palette law (violet only for registry state and primary action), cloud quarry (deep gaps between sections), deep dive (one lifecycle axis shared by every loan view), leather studio (true soft shadow on sheets only).
